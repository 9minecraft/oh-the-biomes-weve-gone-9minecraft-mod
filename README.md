# Oh The Biomes We've Gone — Vibrant Wilds and Towering Forests

Oh The Biomes We've Gone 9minecraft Mod fills your world with 55 brand new biomes, fresh wood types, and unique wildlife. Each zone generates naturally with custom terrain shapes, foliage, and distinct ground blocks. You can gather new building supplies, grow giant trees from saplings, and meet strange wilderness creatures across the land.

Adds 55 distinct biomes that blend naturally into standard world generation.

## What this build is

An unofficial rebuild of **AOCAWOL**, **Corgi Taco**, **Joseph T. McQuigg (JT122406)**, **YaBoiChips**'s original work, kept running on current Minecraft releases by 9Minecraft Studios. Not affiliated with the original author and not endorsed by them.

Most of the porting work sits in the mixin layer. Each Minecraft release moves or renames the methods this mod hooks into, so the hooks are re-pointed and re-tested against that version rather than rewritten. The behaviour underneath is upstream's and is deliberately left alone — that is the part that makes the mod feel right, and it is not ours to redesign.
